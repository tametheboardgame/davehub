# DH-00A — Creative brief

## What we are making

A distinctive digital environment for David's games, stories, musicals, writing, original software, tools, experiments, prototypes and odd ideas. The website is itself another creative project.

**Purpose:** Give everything a place to exist where interested visitors can discover and experience it, without selling services, advertising a career or building a professional 'personal brand'.

## Audience

- People arriving through a direct link to a specific project.
- Curious visitors who want to explore something strange.
- David, archiving the development process as well as the outcomes.

## What should happen in the first visit?

1. A striking, unusual premise becomes apparent within seconds.
2. Something reacts when the visitor interacts.
3. At least one actual project is easy to reach.
4. A curious visitor finds a surprising detail or connection.
5. A goal-directed visitor can skip novelty and get straight to content.

## Content worth celebrating

- Playable games, interactive stories and demonstrations.
- Behind-the-scenes work, development diaries, design decisions and failures.
- Books, scripts, musicals, illustrations and audio for which publication is authorised.
- Technical experiments, using public or synthetic data.
- Abandoned prototypes and unfinished ideas, clearly labelled.

## New conceptual framing

A possible opening shows an **original stylised Dave-like character**, then moves through a playful threshold into an extraordinary representation of the creative mind. The Dave Machine is the inner organising system: games, stories, music, experiments, unfinished ideas and development history become discoverable interconnected experiences. The opening must be skippable and the likeness must not be a photograph or realistically identifying portrait. The precise creative treatment remains subject to concept review.

## Visual tone

Not deliberately ramshackle, collage-like or grotesque. Aim for a coherent, carefully designed and surprising world. The user has explicitly vetoed **Terry Gilliam / Monty Python cut-out animation aesthetics**. See [Visual direction](VISUAL_DIRECTION.md) for detailed exclusions.

## Core visual direction locked 21 October 2026

The approved three-image concept set establishes a stylised illustrated Dave host and a polished fantasy/sci-fi **mindscape of floating worlds, luminous architecture and meaningful category destinations**. **Concept 1, the floating-islands hero, is the owner's favourite and primary reference.** Concepts 2 and 3 are supporting treatments for portal areas and a central observatory/interface. These are reference images, not pixel-perfect finished website screenshots.

Interactive exploration is click/tap and scene selection, **not** a small avatar walking around the world. The visitor can always select **"I want to view this as a menu coz I'm boring"** and enter an intentionally basic 1990s HTML website with the same real projects and links. See [locked concept gallery](LOCKED_CONCEPTS.md) and [retro mode](BORING_MENU.md).

## Explicit non-goals

- Developer portfolio, CV or recruitment pitch.
- Generic Wix-style website, standard portfolio grid or blog theme.
- Terry Gilliam-inspired cut-out animation, grotesque photo collage, old-paper surrealism or default steampunk imagery.
- A 3D world added for spectacle rather than meaningful exploration.
- Tiny player avatars, free-roaming character controls or compulsory walking between projects.
- A 'boring menu' that is just a glossy modern grid instead of a deliberately plain retro HTML experience.
- An interface that hides content behind long loading sequences.
- Publishing material from private repos or work without approval.

**Guiding sentence:** DaveHub is a place you discover, not a profile you scroll.

DaveHub is the working name; **davehub.co.uk has been purchased** according to the owner. No final artwork, hosting or concept has been selected.
