# DH-00A — Creative brief

## What we are making

A distinctive digital environment for David's games, stories, musicals, writing, original software, tools, experiments, prototypes and odd ideas. The website is itself another creative project.

**Purpose:** Give everything a place to exist where interested visitors can discover and experience it, without selling services, advertising a career or building a professional 'personal brand'.

## Audience

- People arriving through a direct link to a specific project.
- Curious visitors who want to explore something strange.
- David, archiving the development process as well as the outcomes.

## What should happen in the first visit?

1. A striking, unusual premise becomes apparent within seconds.
2. Something reacts when the visitor interacts.
3. At least one actual project is easy to reach.
4. A curious visitor finds a surprising detail or connection.
5. A goal-directed visitor can skip novelty and get straight to content.

## Content worth celebrating

- Playable games, interactive stories and demonstrations.
- Behind-the-scenes work, development diaries, design decisions and failures.
- Books, scripts, musicals, illustrations and audio for which publication is authorised.
- Technical experiments, using public or synthetic data.
- Abandoned prototypes and unfinished ideas, clearly labelled.

## Explicit non-goals

- Developer portfolio, CV or recruitment pitch.
- Generic Wix-style website, standard portfolio grid or blog theme.
- A 3D world added for spectacle rather than meaningful exploration.
- An interface that hides content behind long loading sequences.
- Publishing material from private repos or work without approval.

**Guiding sentence:** DaveHub is a place you discover, not a profile you scroll.

DaveHub is a provisional name. No final artwork, brand, domain or concept has been selected.
