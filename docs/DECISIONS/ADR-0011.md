# ADR-0011 — Lock the DaveHub visual and navigation baseline

**Status:** Accepted  
**Decision date:** 27 October 2026  
**Merged reference:** `f822811d68402ab70436839b9b256c90fe5bd40c`  
**Owner approval:** Explicit "lock and merge" after iterative homepage and browser-preview review.

## Decision

1. Preserve the approved cosmic-orbit DaveHub logo and detailed floating-islands background.
2. The homepage is a responsive single-screen illustrated composition, **not** a scrolling card list or a walking-avatar game.
3. Keep exactly **four clickable floating-island destinations**: Writing, Games, Experiments and Projects.
4. Keep exactly **one central Explore DaveHub action**. No duplicated bottom category menu.
5. Explore opens the mystical portal-style directory, visually tied to the homepage background, dividing four principal worlds from Music, The Unfinished Shelf and Trip Down Memory Lane.
6. The illustrated static Giant Dave stands left of the centred Explore action, positioned relative to that button's visible edge with a small target gap, rather than pinned to the viewport edge. The still remains a reduced-motion/fallback candidate while the owner creates the actual animation separately.
7. Page prototypes for deliberately unfinished works and complete musical project worlds are accepted as design directions, including the modern sans-serif typography. Their **fictional** demonstration records do not constitute approval of any real creative material.
8. The locked visual baseline does **not** approve publication of the owner's books, albums, private scripts or historical creations. All such material remains subject to the separate rights and editorial workflow in DH-08.

## Implemented and merged

- PR #16 — DH-07C content-world prototypes
- PR #17 — DH-07D navigation and removal of the redundant bottom menu
- PR #18 — DH-07E static Dave, mystical Explore, and CTA-relative placement

All were merged into `main` in dependency order on 27 October 2026; the final merge received a successful Cloudflare Pages deployment check.

## Consequences

The visual baseline can now serve as a stable host for the approved catalogue. Subsequent work should focus on DH-08 content architecture, approval and publishing, not reopen the front page by default. Any material change to the navigation or Dave staging should be a separately reviewed design decision. A video swap should preserve the same geometry and static fallback without blocking clickable destinations.
