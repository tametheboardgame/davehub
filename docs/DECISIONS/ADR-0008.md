# ADR-0008 - Approve homepage floating-islands background

**Date:** 21 October 2026
**Status:** Accepted

The clean, text-free, UI-free floating-islands image from the Concept 1 refinement is approved as the DaveHub homepage background. Its original 1672 × 941 PNG is the source of truth. A visually faithful SVG with the original PNG embedded is provided for convenience; it is **not a native vector illustration**.

The approved logo, illustrated Dave, Explore button and category navigation must be composited in independent, accessible frontend layers. Responsive cropping and optimised deployment formats will be reviewed in DH-01A. Do not overwrite the approved original.

Reference: [Homepage background](../HOMEPAGE_BACKGROUND.md).
