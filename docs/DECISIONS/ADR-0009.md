# ADR-0009 — Lock Giant Dave key-pose character design

**Date:** 22 October 2026
**Status:** Accepted (character design and poses only)

The owner approved **all four poses** of the Giant Dave concept study generated on 22 October 2026. The result captures the intended character identity and warmth: bald, blue-eyed, short ginger-brown beard, navy hoodie over olive shirt, jeans, confident expressions, cinematic/comic-painterly lighting. The four approved poses are idle, welcoming reach, pointing/attention and open-arm presentation.

The approved four-panel reference is the artistic baseline for DH-06B. It is a **flat RGB concept image**, not an animated rig or a set of transparent independent sprites. Its SHA-256 is `18397610f45669b8bfbb0d5c64e33ff395c7fdf8b565c1598dc03bb635a84930`, dimensions 1774 × 887. The PNG itself remains attached to the ChatGPT conversation and **has not been committed** to GitHub. Preserve the original and upload it when a verified binary-capable pathway is available.

The next stage is to isolate rig-ready illustrated components, test a natural idle/breathing/blink and welcome arm/face movement, and review a working desktop/mobile animation before selecting a runtime or merging a live-site change. Rive is a candidate rather than a binding selection.

Constraints remain: Dave is a host, not a walking avatar; no cut-out-collage or visible puppet joints; maintain the full-screen responsive layout, navigability, quiet defaults and a `prefers-reduced-motion` static fallback.
