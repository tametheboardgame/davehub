# ADR-0005 — Lock approved DaveHub concept art, with Concept 1 leading

**Date:** 21 October 2026  
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Three generated full-resolution concept images constitute the **approved core visual direction**. The owner's favourite is **Concept 1**, featuring a confident stylised Dave in a navy hoodie and olive top, with a luminous floating-islands creative world, a clear invitation to explore and a visible menu alternative.

Concept 2 is a supporting reference for themed portal destinations; Concept 3 for observatory/interior and interface elements. This is **one overall design system, not three ongoing competing themes**.

The illustrated host is based on an approved user-supplied visual character reference: bald/shaved head, ginger-brown beard, strong brows, contemporary illustrated/game-character style. Do not use a photo of the owner.

**Navigation requirement:** No small avatars walking around DaveHub. Visitors activate scenes, portals, destinations or direct links through click/tap/keyboard interaction.

The appearance must not mimic Gilliam / Monty Python cut-out animation, ramshackle steampunk or grotesque collages.

## Preservation

The three original concept PNGs are committed unchanged under `assets/concepts/2026-10-21/`. Matching Google Drive originals are stored in the [DaveHub Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1dezrC1Q5uLgSkeDUWDM6dHH-UbqayufG). See [gallery](../LOCKED_CONCEPTS.md).

## Consequences

Further work refines the **approved direction** rather than producing a new unrelated visual contest. Exact production images, copy, section names, layout, animation, responsive design, implementations and deployment remain open for review.
