# ADR-0003 — Domain and host candidate

- **Date:** 20 October 2026
- **Status:** Domain decided; hosting provisional

## Decision

The owner has purchased **davehub.co.uk**; use it as the primary future website domain. The .com was not purchased due to an unreasonably high asking price. No need to own the .com to proceed.

Cloudflare is a **preferred hosting candidate**, not yet a configured service or final architecture decision. Keep the interactive layer and content system portable until prototypes establish the requirements.

## Implementation pending

- Determine the domain registrar/DNS management path when configuration is needed.
- Choose a deployment service and verify a test deployment.
- Connect the domain and confirm DNS, HTTPS and redirects at launch.
- Do not imply a deployed site or working DNS from domain purchase alone.
