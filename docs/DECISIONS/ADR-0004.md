# ADR-0004 — Creative mindscape and Gilliam-style exclusion

- **Date:** 20 October 2026
- **Status:** Constraints accepted; specific art direction to be explored

## Context

The owner suggested opening with a version of their face (without showing their actual face) and travelling into the mind, where the Dave Machine represents the internal workings. The owner **explicitly does not want a Terry Gilliam cut-out animation / mad collage vibe**.

## Decision

Explore the mindscape premise in DH-01A and as appropriate in other concepts. The entrance character should be invented, stylised and non-photographic; likeness and animation need approval.

Reject Gilliam / Monty Python-style cut-out collage, grotesque body-part surrealism, paper-puppet animation, Victorian scrapbook aesthetics and default junky steampunk. Reinterpret 'machine' as a coherent, unusual internal creative system rather than a pile of random gears.

Keep the entrance optional with direct access to content.

## Consequences

Update all concept briefs, visual references, prompts and prototype reviews to enforce the veto. No final illustrative style, 3D rendering choice or entrance treatment is approved yet.
