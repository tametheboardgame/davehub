# DH-11C.6E.1: Uncrumple the visible scrap

**Stage:** Canonical Writing Room on Cloudflare staging only. **Scope:** Focused user-feedback repair to DH-11C.6E, with no geometry/content changes.

## Reported usability defect

A user-provided laptop screenshot shows the 3D wastepaper experience's initial **crumpled** reading state. The paper itself looks attractive but is not clickable. The only unfold action is in the footer, below the visible viewport because the oversized heading and stage pushed the controls out of sight. There is no visible way to read the idea without scrolling.

## Accepted repair

- The central paper is now a **native HTML button** that can be clicked, tapped, focused and activated using Enter/Space. Its descriptive accessible name makes its purpose clear.
- A prominent button labelled **Uncrumple paper** appears **directly below the crumpled paper**, in the initial visible stage rather than the off-screen footer.
- Either control invokes the **same existing `smooth()`** handler: the physical-looking paper unfolds, the invented text appears, and focus moves to **Turn over**.
- On the opened paper both entry points hide; **Turn over**, **Leave on the floor** and **Crumple and throw back** retain their exact existing behaviour.
- The modal fits within the viewport and separates the scrollable paper stage from its always-visible header and footer. Short laptop heights receive a smaller header and crumple; reduced-motion preferences are honoured. The content can scroll internally if necessary without hiding all the actions.
- All nine fictional samples, actual 3D toss-and-return mechanics, cabinet, bookcase, desk and typewriter are unchanged.
- Opt-in stage `data-uncrumple-direct="true"`; earlier preview renderer/source remains unmodified. Updated CSS/JS are versioned, with the canonical HTML generated by `scripts/render-dh11c6e1-visible-uncrumple.cjs`.

## Visual approval

On staging, select any paper and check that **Uncrumple paper** is visible immediately on an ordinary laptop, and that clicking the paper performs the same action. Check a narrow mobile display and a shorter laptop viewport; turn the note over and throw it back. No production `main` promotion without owner approval.
